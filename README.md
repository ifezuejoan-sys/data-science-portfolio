## About This Portfolio

The projects in this repository demonstrate my approach to exploring datasets, identifying patterns, communicating findings, and translating analysis into practical business recommendations.

## Projects

### 1. Insurance Customer Analytics

**Focus:** Insurance data analysis and exploratory data analysis (EDA).

Explores an insurance dataset to understand customer characteristics, insurance charges, and patterns within the data.

**Key skills:** Data cleaning, descriptive statistics, exploratory data analysis, and data visualization.

**Business application:** Understanding customer segments and insurance cost patterns to support data-informed business decisions.

### 2. Credit Default Prediction

**Focus:** Financial risk analysis and classification.

Examines credit card customer data and compares machine-learning models to identify customers at risk of default.

**Key skills:** Data preparation, class imbalance analysis, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, model evaluation, and classification threshold analysis.

**Business application:** Understanding credit risk and evaluating models that could support lending-risk assessment.

### 3. Nigeria Economic Indicators and Sales Analysis

**Focus:** Economic indicators and business performance.

Explores Nigerian inflation indicators alongside an illustrative sales dataset to practise comparing economic trends with revenue and unit sales.

**Key skills:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, trend analysis, revenue calculations, and correlation analysis.

**Business application:** Demonstrating how businesses can investigate economic conditions alongside sales performance when reviewing pricing and demand.

### 4. Nigerian Retail Sales Analysis

**Focus:** Sales performance and profitability.

Uses simulated retail transactions across selected Nigerian cities to compare product revenue, sales volume, profit, and profit margins.

**Key skills:** Data aggregation, sales analysis, profit calculations, product comparison, city-level analysis, and visualization.

**Business application:** Supporting product performance reviews, pricing analysis, and commercial decision-making.

### 5. Customer Churn Analysis and Prediction

**Focus:** Customer retention and predictive analytics.

Uses simulated customer records to explore churn patterns and compare Logistic Regression and Random Forest classifiers.

**Key skills:** Exploratory analysis, classification, model evaluation, feature importance, and business recommendations.

**Business application:** Demonstrating analytical approaches businesses can use to investigate customer retention and potential churn risks.

### 6. Loan Default Risk Analysis and Prediction

**Focus:** Borrower risk and financial analytics.

Uses simulated loan applicant data to examine repayment outcomes and compare machine-learning models for default classification.

**Key skills:** Financial data analysis, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, classification metrics, feature importance, and risk interpretation.

**Business application:** Exploring how borrower characteristics and model evaluation can contribute to credit-risk analysis.

## Important Data Notes

The projects use a combination of public datasets and simulated or illustrative data.

- The credit default project uses public credit card customer data.
- The insurance project uses an insurance charges dataset.
- The Nigeria economic and sales project includes illustrative sales records; economic figures should be verified against official publications before drawing real-world conclusions.
- The retail sales, customer churn, and loan default projects use simulated data for learning and demonstration.

Results from simulated datasets are not presented as evidence of actual Nigerian market conditions or validated real-world predictive performance.
