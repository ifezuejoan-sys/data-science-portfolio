# Insurance Customer & Cost Analytics

## Project Overview
This project explores a public medical insurance dataset to identify patterns in insurance charges and examine differences across customer segments.

## Business Questions
- How do average insurance charges vary by smoking status?
- What patterns exist across age, BMI, sex, region, and number of children?
- What data-quality issues should be considered before analysis?

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Key Findings
- The dataset contains 1,338 records and seven columns, with no missing values detected.
- One duplicate row was identified for further investigation.
- Average charges for smokers were $32,050.23, compared with $8,434.27 for non-smokers.
- Smokers' average charges were approximately 3.8 times those of non-smokers.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the factors associated with higher insurance charges.
- Examine customer characteristics collectively rather than relying on a single variable.
- Explore cost forecasting and risk-analysis methods.
- Validate findings using representative data before making business or pricing decisions.

## Limitations
This analysis uses a public educational dataset. The results describe associations and do not establish causation. They are not actuarial pricing recommendations.

## Project Status
Exploratory analysis in progress. Further visualisation, validation, and documentation will strengthen the findings.
